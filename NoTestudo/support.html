Common questions

Location isn't tracking. Check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → NoTestudo and choose "Always" for background visit tracking, or "While Using" for foreground-only.

Delete my account. In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your visits, places, goals, groups, friends and profile from our servers first, then clears the data on your device. Apple and RevenueCat retain subscription records under their own policies.

Manage my subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple — manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.
